A voice booking layer that keeps working when the whole festival arrives

For shrines, temple boards and large venues with festival-day rushes: pilgrims book a fair slot by talking in their own language, and one volunteer approves the rush switch by voice.

Our cost

Rs 3.5

per voice booking, from Sarvam public prices (checked 25 Nov 2026).

The shrine pays

per voice booking, with a minimum. The pilgrim pays Rs 0.

If every big shrine bought it

~Rs 30 cr

a year. Our estimate: 15 mega shrines and about 300 mid-size.

Pilgrims never pay. No ads. No data sales.

What a shrine gets

  • Booking by voice in Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi and English, with the slot read back.
  • An assistant that answers pilgrims and volunteers about timings, the way, water, medical help and help for elders, from the shrine's own facts.
  • A swappable venue pack: any shrine or venue loads its own pack, with no code change.
  • Rush protection: NudgeBee finds the cause, a volunteer says yes, Flagsmith switches to staggered slots and a lighter page.
  • A walk-in counter where a volunteer books for pilgrims without a phone.

Cost to serve

  • About Rs 3.5 per voice booking: speech-to-text, text-to-speech, translation, the language model and retries.
  • Example: 100,000 pilgrims a day with 15% using voice is about Rs 52,500 a day of Sarvam cost (estimate).

Estimates use public prices and our own assumptions; see business/BUSINESS.md in the repository. The crowd in the demo is simulated and no shrine has adopted it yet.
