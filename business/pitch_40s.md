# Darshan Rush: 40-second money pitch

"Every season, a shrine's booking system works until ten thousand people arrive at once. In Tirupati, six people died in a token crush in January 2025. We sell the layer that keeps working when that happens: you speak in your own language, you get a fair slot, and one volunteer approves the switch by voice. It costs us about three and a half rupees per booking. The shrine pays ten. The pilgrim pays nothing, ever. No ads, no data sales. India makes 1.4 billion pilgrim trips a year, and we need fifteen big shrines to be a business. We start with one free pilot on one festival day."

## One slide, three numbers
1. **Rs 3.5** our cost per voice booking (Sarvam public prices, 25 Nov 2026)
2. **Rs 10** the shrine pays. **Rs 0** the pilgrim pays.
3. **~Rs 30 crore** a year if every big shrine bought it (our estimate: 15 mega + ~300 mid-size)

Honesty line if asked: the crowd in the demo is simulated and no shrine has adopted it yet. The prices and costs are public; demand is proven only by the free pilot.
