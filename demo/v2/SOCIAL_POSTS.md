## LinkedIn

Spent today at Festive Hacks (WeMakeDevs at WeAreDevelopers, Bengaluru) building Darshan Rush.

The idea started with one person: a 68-year-old pilgrim who speaks only Telugu and has never booked anything online. She taps once and talks. She gets a darshan slot read back to her, can ask how far the next stop is or where the water is, and sees a simple map with "You are here".

The harder part was the day everyone arrives at once. We made the booking service really crash on Kubernetes under a rush. NudgeBee finds the cause, the app asks the volunteer at the counter in Malayalam whether to switch to a lighter mode, she says yes out loud, a duty officer approves in NudgeBee, and two Flagsmith flags flip. Same rush, no crash. Sarvam AI does all the listening and speaking.

One honest thing I learned: short answers are the hardest for speech. A full sentence in casual Telugu came through fine. A one-word "okay" in Malayalam often did not. So the volunteer screen also has big Yes and No buttons, and we ask people to say two words.

The crowd is simulated and no shrine uses this yet. The crash and the fix are real.

Thanks to WeMakeDevs, WeAreDevelopers, Flagsmith, NudgeBee and Sarvam AI.
